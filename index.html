Useful links

A short list, kept short on purpose — places I actually send people.

Ministry in Sydney

  • Sydney Anglican Chinese Ministry 悉尼聖公會華文事工

    The network of Sydney Anglican churches with Chinese-language ministry — Mandarin and Cantonese congregations across the diocese, and the One For All conference.

  • St Paul's Anglican Church Kogarah

    The parish I serve. Two congregations in parallel — one English-speaking and all-together, one Mandarin-speaking — along with growth groups, youth and children's ministry, Easy English classes and a food pantry.

Resources

  • Multi-Language Bible Resource Hub — Anglicare Sydney

    Bibles, gospel explanations and discipleship material in twelve languages — Arabic, Cantonese, Farsi, German, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Nepali, Russian, Spanish and Tamil — plus links to 53 Australian Indigenous languages and hundreds more. Also practical guidance on translating, interpreting and choosing which language to use. The first place to look if someone walks into your church and English is not going to reach them.
